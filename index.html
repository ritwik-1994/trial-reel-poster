how this works

A two-minute read before your first batch.

  1. Three spaces, on the left rail. Library is every reel on @aware._wolf_ with its numbers. New is where you set up and queue reels. Queue shows what's scheduled, posting, live, or needs attention.
  2. What a Trial Reel is. Instagram shows it only to people who don't follow @aware._wolf_. Followers don't see it unless it's shared to them later.
  3. New reels. Drop 1 to 4 videos. They line up in the filmstrip on the left. Click one to edit its caption, cover frame (drag the slider), what happens after the trial, and when it posts. A red ✕ on a reel means something needs fixing.
  4. After the trial: I'll decide later keeps it with non-followers until someone shares it from the Instagram app. Share automatically if it does well lets Instagram share it to followers by itself.
  5. Review & queue. Nothing reaches Instagram until you press the final button in the review window. Posting takes 1 to 3 minutes per reel.
  6. Library. Each tile shows one number; pick which with the lens (Views, Share %, Avg watch…). ▲ means 25%+ better than this account usually does, ▼ means 25%+ worse. Click a tile for everything about it. Use ← → to move between reels.
  7. Three things only the Instagram app can do: Hide like count, Translations and Share to followers. Each reel's panel has the exact taps; tick them when done and the whole team sees it. The small circle on a tile counts what's left.
  8. Shortcuts: 1 Library · 2 New · 3 Queue · ? this help · Esc close.
